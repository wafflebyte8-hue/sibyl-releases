Portraits of the Legends and Creators bots
=========================================

Each portrait is an illustration painted by a program (cutout of the person, then repainted with brush strokes) from one photograph.
The photographs come from Wikimedia Commons. Each illustration is a derivative of its photograph and is shared under the same licence as that photograph;
where the photograph is CC BY or CC BY-SA, credit the photographer as listed below. The people shown are not affiliated with Sibyl and have not endorsed it.
